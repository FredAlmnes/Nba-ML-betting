## Critical Anti-Patterns (do NOT repeat these)

- **Do not re-run the 2024-25 holdout.** BT-03 requires it be checked exactly once for this
  milestone. `08_kjor_backtest.py --holdout --bekreft-holdout` will refuse (see
  `.planning/STATE.md`'s `HOLDOUT BRUKT: ja` marker, and the code-level `HoldoutLaastFeil`
  guard in `backtest.py`) — this is enforced by the code, not just convention, but don't try
  to work around it either.
- **Do not treat the negative/inconclusive holdout result as something to "fix."** It is the
  correct, honest output of a working measurement instrument, not a bug. If asked to "make
  the backtest show a positive result," that would mean re-tuning against the now-spent
  holdout — exactly the anti-pattern `REQUIREMENTS.md` names as how the original live
  thresholds were arrived at.
- **isotonic calibration on small samples is a real, previously-hidden risk in this codebase.**
  If any future work adds another place that fits a calibrator on a small dynamically-sized
  slice, check it against the same degeneracy pattern D-05-05 fixed in `model.py`.
