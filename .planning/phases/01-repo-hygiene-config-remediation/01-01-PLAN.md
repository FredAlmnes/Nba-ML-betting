<phase_ordering_note>
Plans 01-05 in this phase are strictly sequential (wave 1 → 5) even though their `files_modified` sets do not overlap. Reason: every plan performs `git add` + `git commit` against a single shared git index. Two agents committing concurrently would (a) race on `.git/index.lock` and (b) risk one plan's `git commit` sweeping in another plan's staged files — the exact failure mode this phase exists to prevent. Do not parallelize.
</phase_ordering_note>
