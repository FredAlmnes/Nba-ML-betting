<objective>
Build `metrics.py` — the pure, zero-I/O reporting layer that turns a completed backtest bet
ledger into the headline numbers BT-04 and BT-06 require, plus `tests/test_metrics.py`
asserting every one of them against values that can be worked out by hand.

This plan writes no loop, no I/O and no orchestration. It writes arithmetic that later plans
depend on being right: plan 05-08 calls `oppsummer_ledger()` to fill `manifest.json`, plan
05-09 calls the same functions once per Kelly fraction for `kelly_sweep.json`, and plan 05-12's
human-verify checkpoint judges plausibility using exactly these numbers. If the ROI
denominator, the drawdown peak anchor or the CLV sign is wrong here, every downstream number
is wrong in a way that looks entirely plausible.

Two constraints are load-bearing and non-negotiable:
- **No `scipy`.** The Wilson z-score is a hardcoded `1.96`. `scipy` exists transitively in the
  venv but is deliberately absent from `requirements.txt` (05-RESEARCH.md §Standard Stack →
  Supporting, and §Alternatives Considered). Importing it would silently add an undeclared
  runtime dependency that works on this machine and fails on a fresh clone.
- **One vig-removal implementation in the repo, ever.** CLV reuses
  `strategy.fjern_vigorish` on both snapshots. 05-RESEARCH.md §Don't Hand-Roll names a second
  vig implementation as the way Pitfall 4 (vig-removal errors manufacturing fake edge)
  reopens in a new place.

Purpose: BT-04 requires a confidence interval attached to *every* headline number precisely
because the expected bet count on the train/calibrate slice (~190-360, per 05-RESEARCH.md
Pattern 5) sits at or below the "statistically inconclusive" line. A wide CI is the correct,
honest output here — the engine's job is to show it, not hide it behind a single ROI figure.
Output: `metrics.py` (nine pure functions) and `tests/test_metrics.py`.

<clv_sign_convention>
**Read this before writing `beregn_clv`. It is the single most misreadable number in the phase.**

`05-CONTEXT.md` (corrected 2026-08-24 — an earlier draft had the operands reversed, which
inverted the sign) locks the CLV formula as *"the vig-free implied probability at the
closing snapshot minus the vig-free implied probability at the bet-time snapshot"*. That
orientation is the locked decision and **must be implemented exactly as written** — do not
silently flip it.

This orientation matches the everyday "positive CLV = good" reading: getting a *better*
price than the close means your bet-time implied probability was *lower* than the closing
implied probability, so **a POSITIVE value means the bet beat the closing line** — consistent
with BT-06's framing of CLV as "a faster-converging signal of genuine edge."

Worked example this plan's test pins: bet home at 2.00 (vig-free 0.500), game closes at 1.50
(vig-free 0.667). You got a clearly better price, and the locked formula returns
`0.667 - 0.500 = +0.167`.

Because BT-06 wants CLV read as a *signal of genuine edge*, the aggregate function must also
return `andel_slo_closing` — the share of bets with `clv > 0` — so plan 05-08 and plan 05-12's
human-verify checkpoint have one field whose direction is unambiguous regardless of sign
convention. That field is an addition to the locked decision, not a substitute for it: both
are reported.
