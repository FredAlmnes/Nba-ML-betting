<objective>
Spend the holdout. Once.

Phases 1 through 4 removed every confirmed risk that would have poisoned a backtest number.
Plans 05-01 through 05-11 built the engine and proved its parts correct. Plan 05-12 ran it over
2022-23 and 2023-24, showed the developer the numbers, and froze every threshold and staking
decision. All of that exists for the sake of one command, executed one time, against a slice of
data no decision in this project has ever seen: the 2024-25 season.

This plan runs that command. Before it does, it holds a blocking checkpoint whose job is not to
be procedurally present but to make the irreversibility genuinely understood — because after
this run, 2024-25 is no longer out-of-sample for this project, and no amount of care afterwards
restores it. Deleting the run directory does not undo it. The knowledge is in the developer's
head, and that is precisely what BT-03's lock protects.

Then it writes down what the run means: the numbers with their confidence intervals, and the
before/after comparison against the configuration that took the paper bankroll from 1 000 kr to
74,88 kr. That comparison is this milestone's actual payoff — the evidence
`.planning/PROJECT.md` names as the Core Value gate. It has to be honest in both directions: a
holdout result that fails to show positive validated ROI is the gate working, not the plan
failing, and this plan must be as capable of recording that verdict as the happy one.

Purpose: BT-03's "checked exactly once, after all threshold/parameter decisions are frozen"
becomes true only when the single check has actually happened and been recorded somewhere a
future session will find it. BT-05's "enabling a clear before/after comparison against the
current losing live configuration" becomes true only when that comparison is written.
Output: `05-HOLDOUT-RESULTAT.md`, one `backtests/<run_id>/` directory whose manifest reads
`type: "holdout"`, a `HOLDOUT BRUKT: ja` block in `.planning/STATE.md`, a closed-out
`05-VALIDATION.md`, and BT-01..BT-07 marked complete in `REQUIREMENTS.md` and `ROADMAP.md`.

This plan writes no production code. It runs one already-shipped CLI and edits five planning
documents.

- **`nba_features.csv` integrity:** 3 638 rows, 480 unique values of `GAME_DATE_HJEMME`,
  spanning `2022-10-24` .. `2025-04-13`. Plan 05-12's Task 1 ran against this same file and
  forbade regenerating it; the same prohibition applies here, and the row/date counts are the
  cheap check that it has not moved underneath the freeze.
- **The holdout slice** (`GAME_DATE_HJEMME >= "2024-10-01"`): **1 225 games** across **162
  unique dates**, first date **2024-10-22**, last date **2025-04-13**, spanning exactly **7
  calendar months** (2024-10 with 10 dates, 2024-11 with 28, 2024-12 with 27, 2025-01 with 31,
  2025-02 with 23, 2025-03 with 31, 2025-04 with 12).
- **The tuning slice** (the complement): 2 413 games across 318 dates — which reproduces plan
  05-12's `periode.datoer_totalt` of 318 exactly, and is the cross-check that the 162/1 225
  numbers above are the right complement and not a coincidence.
- **Therefore the holdout run's expected counters are:** `periode.datoer_totalt` **162**,
  `periode.datoer_behandlet` **162**, `periode.kamper_totalt` **1225**,
  `datakvalitet.retreninger` **7**,
  `datakvalitet.datoer_hoppet_over_for_lite_treningsgrunnlag` **0**,
  `periode.til_dato` **"2025-04-13"**, `periode.fra_dato` **"2024-10-22"**.
- **Why the warm-up skip is 0 here and 15 in the tuning run.** The training window is expanding
  from 2022-10-24, so every holdout date already has at least 2 413 games strictly before it —
  far above `min_treningskamper` (100). A non-zero value in that counter would mean the
  expanding window was not expanding, which is a defect, not a boundary condition.
- **Why `datoer_totalt` must read 162 and not 480.** `08_kjor_backtest.py` refuses `--fra`/`--til`
  alongside `--holdout`, so `klargjor_backtestdata` loads all 480 dates and
  `kjor_endelig_holdout_backtest` narrows them itself. A `datoer_totalt` of 480 means the
  narrowing did not happen and the run scored tuning dates as if they were holdout dates.
- **Closing-snapshot gap.** Exactly one of the two known gap games falls in this slice:
  `2025-01-09` DAL(1610612742) vs POR(1610612757). So `kamper_uten_closing_snapshot` can be at
  most **1**, and at most one bet can carry `bets_uten_clv`. (The other gap game, `2023-03-11`
  PHX vs SAC, sits in the tuning slice and belongs to plan 05-12's run.)
- **The live losing baseline, and what is left of it.** `.planning/PROJECT.md` §Context records
  that the tracked virtual bankroll fell from 1 000 kr to **74,88 kr** under the currently-running
  configuration; `.planning/codebase/CONCERNS.md` records the same figure as a ~92,5 % drawdown
  read from `bankroll.json`'s `historikk`. **That history no longer exists on disk.** Verified
  2026-08-26: `bankroll.json` now reads `saldo: 1000.0` with an empty `historikk`, and
  `bets.json` is an empty list. Both are gitignored, so there is no committed copy to recover.
  The before-side of the comparison therefore has a bankroll endpoint and nothing else — no bet
  count, no win rate, no measured drawdown series, no CLV.
- **A number that must NOT be used as the baseline.** `KALIBRERING_RAPPORT.md` /
  `ENDRINGER_SUMMARY.txt` claim a 17 % win rate and −67 % ROI for the pre-fix threshold values.
  Phase 1 marked both documents SUPERSEDED and never-deployed, and their numbers were never
  reproduced by anything in this repo. They may be named as a superseded historical claim; they
  may not become a row in the before/after table.
- **Cost.** No Odds API credits are spent — the engine reads only the already-archived
  `odds_arkiv.db`. 7 retrains at roughly 0,4 s per XGBoost + isotonic fit; the whole run is
  seconds, not minutes. Nothing about the cost justifies hesitating, and nothing about the
  cheapness makes the run repeatable.
